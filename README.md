# The Woman at the Edge of the Map

**Poems, Memories, Mythic Files**  
*Amy Laird · 2026*

https://amy2213.github.io/The-Woman-At-The-Edge-Of-The-Map/index.html
---

> *For every version of me that got me this far. And for the ones who never made it.*

---

A collection of poetry, memoir fragments, and mythic prose across six parts:

- **Part I: The Edge** — Memory, survival, the cost of passing
- **Part II: Seekers and Shadows** — Ritual, identity, the internal wilderness
- **Part III: The Reckoning** — Grief, anger, the body's accounting
- **Part IV: The Myth** — Archival documents, testimonies, the self as case file
- **Part V: The Return** — Diagnosis, incarceration, reclaiming language
- **Part VI: Personal Letters, Closings, and Artifacts** — Letters unsent and sent, the ordinary miracle

*Content note: suicide, abuse, incarceration.*
