## Approved system

Phase 1 permanently establishes:

- **Creative direction:** The Living Map
- **Visual tone:** contemporary, literary, atmospheric, spacious, and alive
- **Rejected tone:** gothic, funereal, antique, parchment-based, black-and-gold, or trauma-saturated
- **Primary foundations:** ivory, white, ink blue, slate, and mist
- **Section accents:** horizon blue, indigo, coral, sage, sea glass, lavender gray, and restrained saffron
- **Typography:** Newsreader, Inter, and IBM Plex Mono
- **Reading priority:** comfortable long-form reading over decorative effects
- **Motion:** restrained, optional, and reduced-motion compliant
- **Navigation:** stable direct links, normal browser history, canonical previous/next order, and a separate Coda destination
- **Accessibility baseline:** WCAG 2.2 AA targets, keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic hierarchy, 44px touch targets, and 200% text resizing

## Implementation transfer

The approved landing-page prototype and the remaining representative screens become implementation tasks in Phases 2 and 3. Owner approval locks the system without requiring the obsolete single-file website to be fully reskinned first.

## Change control

Any change to the locked palette, typography, visual direction, section progression, or accessibility baseline requires an explicit design change request. Routine responsive refinements that preserve the approved system do not require reopening Phase 1.
